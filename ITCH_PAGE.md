# HUSH
**A town fell silent when one girl stopped playing.**

At the Willowmere festival, Lark froze halfway through her song. Someone laughed. She ran away.
The next morning, silence had a shape.

You are Wren, her best friend. Carry a little brass bell through a colourless market, golden windmill fields, a flooded concert hall and a silent clock tower. Wake the town. Find the pages of an unfinished song. Listen to what everyone remembers — including yourself.

- Four connected chapters, hidden song pages and a chapter journal.
- Bell combos, gentle platforming, gliding and singing windmills.
- A rolling fog chase, drifting rafts and swinging chandeliers.
- Four musical finales and three endings.
- Procedural storybook characters and scenery, with original generated effects.
- The full Jason Grace theme in the true ending, with unchanged audio samples.

Desktop keyboard: A/D move · Space jump/glide · J ring the bell · S/K stomp · E talk · Tab journal · Esc menu · M mute. Click the menu and speaker icons for sound settings. Collect all 12 song pages and listen through the final silence.

## Upload setup
Upload HUSH_itch_browser.zip as HTML, mark it playable in browser, choose 1280×720 and enable fullscreen. It is single-threaded WebGL 2. Add HUSH_Windows.zip as a Windows download. Keep the Godot source ZIP as an optional source download.
